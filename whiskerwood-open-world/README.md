# WHISKERWOOD — A Cat's Little Open World

A cozy top-down open-world cat life simulator built with HTML Canvas, CSS and vanilla JavaScript.

## Play
Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No installation, server, build step, or dependencies required.

## Explore
- Woodland village with distinct garden, forest, meadow, pond, market and cottage areas
- Free movement across a large scrolling map with a camera that follows your cat
- Friendly animal NPCs with dialogue and a small quest chain
- Collectible fish treats, golden acorns and wildflowers
- Interactive home, pond, fountain, market, benches, snacks and toys
- Hunger, energy and happiness stats
- Sprinting, meowing, playtime, time-of-day cycle and minimap
- Cozy custom HUD, start screen, interaction panels and responsive touch controls

## Controls
- WASD / arrow keys: move
- Shift: sprint (uses energy)
- E: interact with nearby characters and objects
- Space: meow

On touchscreen, swipe to move and tap to interact.

## Quest
Help Milo the Mouse find three golden acorns, return them to him, then find a toy for playtime. After that, explore freely.

## GitHub Pages
1. Create a repository and upload `index.html`, `game.js`, and `README.md` to the root.
2. Go to **Settings → Pages**.
3. Select **Deploy from a branch**, choose `main` and `/ (root)`, then save.
4. Open your published Pages URL.
